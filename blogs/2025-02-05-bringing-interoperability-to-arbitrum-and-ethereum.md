Outlines Arbitrum's interoperability vision featuring a Universal Intent Engine enabling cross-chain swaps and transfers in under three seconds. Plans include standardized message formats, broadcast standards, fast settlement, and intent dissemination feeds, with the Arbitrum Foundation's Solver Loan Program bootstrapping liquidity across chains, targeted for Q1 and Q3 2025.
