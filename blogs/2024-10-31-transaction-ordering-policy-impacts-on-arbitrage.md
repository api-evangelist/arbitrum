Research analysis comparing transaction ordering policies' effects on arbitrage profitability across First-Come-First-Serve (FCFS), Priority Gas Auction (PGA), and FCFS with time advantage. Simulations show FCFS with time advantage generates 47.7% more profits than PGA for high-volatility pairs, informing design considerations for Timeboost.
