Retrospective on Prysm's eight-year evolution as an Ethereum consensus client. Details how 37 unique contributors worked across 12 releases to implement the Pectra hard fork, with changes to validator infrastructure and blob throughput, plus upcoming developments like PeerDAS and the Fusaka upgrade scheduled for later in 2025.
