Announcement of a collaboration with Simcluster, an AI-powered social gaming platform at the intersection of crypto and artificial intelligence. Simcluster uses a gamified system with "concepts" as composable building blocks, letting users create and earn a virtual "clout" currency, with plans to bring its concept registry onto Arbitrum as part of Onchain Labs' focus on consumer-facing applications.
